# Prompt Engineering Experiments

## Project: AI Study Assistant

This file contains practical prompt-engineering experiments for an AI Study Assistant.

The experiments demonstrate how prompt structure, context, examples, rules, and refinement can influence the quality and usefulness of AI-generated responses.

---

## Experiment 1: Basic Prompt

### Objective

Test how an AI responds to a simple academic request without additional instructions.

### Prompt

Explain Newton's Laws of Motion.

### What We Are Testing

- Clarity of the response
- Level of detail
- Organization
- Use of examples
- Suitability for a Diploma Engineering student

---

## Experiment 2: Zero-Shot Prompt

### Objective

Test a more specific prompt without providing examples of the expected answer.

### Prompt

You are an engineering tutor.

Explain Newton's Laws of Motion to a Diploma Engineering student.

Explain each law separately using simple language.

For each law, provide:
1. Definition
2. Simple explanation
3. One practical engineering-related example

Use headings and bullet points.

End with 5 practice questions.

### What We Are Testing

Whether clear instructions and role prompting can produce a more structured response without providing examples.

---

## Experiment 3: Few-Shot Prompt

### Objective

Test whether providing an example helps guide the AI toward a desired response format.

### Prompt

You are an engineering tutor helping Diploma Engineering students.

Follow this response pattern:

Example:

Topic: Friction

Definition: Friction is a force that opposes relative motion between surfaces.

Simple Explanation: When two surfaces try to move against each other, friction acts against that motion.

Practical Example: Friction between brake pads and a brake disc helps a vehicle slow down.

Now use the same structure to explain:

Newton's First Law
Newton's Second Law
Newton's Third Law

For each law, include:
- Definition
- Simple Explanation
- Practical Engineering Example

Use simple language.

### What We Are Testing

Whether an example can guide the AI toward a consistent response structure.

## Experiment 4: CARE Framework Prompt

### Objective

Apply the CARE framework to create a structured prompt.

### Context

The learner is a Diploma Engineering student studying basic physics concepts.

### Ask

Explain Newton's Laws of Motion and help the learner understand their practical applications.

### Rules

- Use simple and clear language.
- Explain each law separately.
- Include a definition for each law.
- Include one practical engineering example for each law.
- Use headings and bullet points.
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
- End with 5 practice questions.

### Examples

Use practical examples related to engineering and everyday situations where appropriate.

### Persona

Act as an experienced engineering tutor who explains difficult concepts in a simple and student-friendly way.

### Complete CARE Prompt

You are an experienced engineering tutor helping a Diploma Engineering student.

The student is learning Newton's Laws of Motion and needs a simple explanation with practical applications.

Explain Newton's First, Second, and Third Laws separately.

For each law:
1. Give the definition.
2. Explain it in simple language.
3. Give one practical engineering example.

Use clear headings and bullet points.

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

At the end, provide 5 practice questions to test the student's understanding.

Use practical examples wherever appropriate.

## Experiment 5: Prompt Refinement

### Initial Prompt

Explain Newton's Laws of Motion.

### Problems With the Initial Prompt

The prompt does not specify:

- Who the learner is
- The required level of explanation
- The desired response structure
- Whether examples are required
- How the learner's understanding should be tested

### Refined Prompt

You are an experienced engineering tutor helping a Diploma Engineering student.

Explain Newton's Laws of Motion using simple, student-friendly language.

For each law:
- Provide the definition.
- Explain the concept simply.
- Give one practical engineering example.

Use headings and bullet points.

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

At the end, provide 5 practice questions with answers.

### Refinement Goal

Improve the usefulness, consistency, and learner focus of the AI response by adding context, role, rules, and a clear output structure.
